**Personal project** · November 2026–present · In progress

[XGO-Duck](https://projecthub.arduino.cc/luwudynamics/xgo-duck-a-robot-duck-that-learned-to-walk-2ee980) is an open-source bipedal robot duck from Luwu Dynamics, based on the Microduck design. I am building one myself.

## The robot

* **Body:** 3D-printed parts driven by 15 Feetech serial bus servos.
* **Electronics:** an Arduino UNO Q with the Luwu Dynamics expansion board and an inertial measurement unit.
* **Walking:** a neural network policy runs on the robot 50 times per second and sets the servo targets, so the gait is learned, not hand-programmed.

## The build

The build covers 3D printing the parts, assembling the legs, body and head, setting up and calibrating the servos, and getting the walking policy running on the robot.
